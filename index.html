ABOUT ME

Bioinformatics Research Assistant

Data Engineering • Bioinformatics • Research Software Engineering

I am currently working as a Bioinformatics Research Assistant at the Minderoo OceanOmics Centre, The University of Western Australia, where I develop bioinformatics workflows, automate research pipelines, manage biological datasets, and build scalable software solutions that support genomics and environmental DNA research.

My work combines software engineering, data engineering, cloud technologies, database design, and bioinformatics to transform raw biological data into reliable and reproducible research outputs. I enjoy solving complex problems by developing efficient workflows that improve data quality, automation, and scientific collaboration.

I have experience working with Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, R, AWS, Git, GitHub, Power BI, Databricks, REST APIs, FAIR Data, metadata standards, ETL pipelines, relational database design, and scientific data management.

My long-term goal is to become a leading Bioinformatics Data Engineer, designing large-scale research platforms that bridge biology, software engineering, artificial intelligence, and cloud computing.
